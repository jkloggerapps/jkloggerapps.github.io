Privacy Policy

App Name: Personal Link Manager

Effective Date: May 21, 2026

Personal Link Manager ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") helps users save, organize, manage, back up, and restore web links locally on their device while minimizing data collection.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Store in the App

The App allows you to create and manage content including:

  • Web links (URLs)
  • Link titles
  • Categories and organization data
  • Preview images and website favicons downloaded from webpages
  • Backup files created by the App
  • Imported data from JSON or HTML files

This information is stored locally on your device.

Optional Security PIN

The App provides an optional PIN lock feature for opening the App and opening the Settings screen.

  • Your PIN is stored securely in encrypted form.
  • Your actual PIN is not stored or transmitted.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All link-related information is stored locally on your device using a local database.
Stored data may include:

  • Saved links
  • Categories
  • Preview thumbnails
  • Website favicon images
  • Backup ZIP files
  • Imported/exported files
Important: Link data, thumbnails, exported files, shared files, and backup ZIP files are not encrypted by the App. Only the optional PIN credential is encrypted. Users are responsible for protecting exported and backup files.

3. Preview Images and Website Metadata

To display previews for saved links, the App may:

  • Re-download webpages directly from the original website
  • Retrieve webpage titles
  • Retrieve preview images
  • Retrieve favicon images

Processing of webpage content happens locally within the App. The App does not send your saved links or browsing information to any external API or server for metadata extraction.

4. Backup, Export, Import, and Sharing

The App allows users to:

  • Create backups as ZIP files
  • Restore backups
  • Export data as JSON
  • Export data as HTML
  • Import JSON or HTML files
  • Share exported files

Because exported and backup files are not encrypted, anyone with access to those files may be able to view their contents.

5. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location information
  • App interaction information

Users may remove advertisements through an in-app purchase.
For more information about how Google processes data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Policies

6. In-App Purchases

The App uses Google Play Billing to process purchases for removing ads.

  • Payments are processed by Google.
  • The App does not collect or store payment card information.

7. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not upload or export your saved links, categories, thumbnails, backup files, or imported content to our servers.

Third-party services used:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

8. Permissions

Depending on Android version and device capabilities, the App may request permissions for:

  • Selecting files for import
  • Saving backup/export files
  • Reading restore/import files
  • Network access for downloading webpage previews and favicons

Permissions are used only for intended functionality.

9. GDPR and US State Privacy Rights

Where applicable under GDPR and US privacy laws, users may have rights to:

  • Access information
  • Request deletion
  • Export information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Since most information remains stored locally on the device, users can generally manage or delete data directly within the App. The App utilizes a consent management platform to gather and manage your preferences regarding personalized advertising. You can update or revoke these permissions at any time through the App's settings.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Data Retention

Data remains stored locally until:

  • You delete it
  • You uninstall the App
  • You remove exported or backup files

To completely delete all data associated with the App, users can clear the App's storage in their Android system settings or uninstall the App. Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes become effective upon publication.
